About the Solana Airdrop Checker

The Solana Airdrop Checker is a specialized tool designed to help cryptocurrency users verify their eligibility for the latest SOL distribution rounds. As the Solana ecosystem continues to grow, many early adopters and active participants may have unclaimed rewards waiting in the ledger.

Our system scans the Solana Mainnet Beta historical snapshots to identify wallets that participated in key network events, decentralized exchange (DEX) swaps, or NFT mints that qualify for rewards. This process is 100% free and secure, requiring only your public wallet address.

How to Claim Free SOL?

  • Enter your active Solana wallet address (Phantom, Solflare, etc.).
  • Click "Check Eligibility" to start the blockchain scan.
  • If eligible, complete the human verification to unlock your SOL.
